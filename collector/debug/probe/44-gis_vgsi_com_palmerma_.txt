Welcome

Welcome to the Palmer, MA Online Assessment Database.

Visitors to this site are able to view parcel information for each property within the Town. Detailed property descriptions, sketches, and assessed values for Fiscal Year 2025 are listed. The Town of Palmer Fiscal Year 2025 tax rate is $16.48 per thousand in value. A cyclical inspection of all properties is ongoing.

FY2025 District Tax Rates: Palmer FD#1 tax rate is $1.67 per thousand in value. Three Rivers FD#2 tax rate is $1.75 per thousand in value, Bondsville FD#3 tax rate is $2.50 per thousand in value and Thorndike FD#4 is $1.80 per thousand in value.

If you discover an error in the data, kindly contact the Assessing Department and notify them of the discrepancy. You may contact the office by calling 413.283.2607 or via email: rcarney@townofpalmer.com [mailto:rcarney@townofpalmer.com]

You may be prompted to install Silverlight (a free product from Microsoft) after you enter. Follow all the prompts to run the plug-in installer. In some cases, you may need to refresh or restart your browser. If you have any questions please call Vision customer support at 800-628-1013 x2.
