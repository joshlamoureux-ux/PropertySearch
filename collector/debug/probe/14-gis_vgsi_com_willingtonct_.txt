Welcome

Welcome to the Willington, CT Online Assessment Database.

The
Town of Willington, CT contracted with Municipal Valuation Services,
LLC to assist with the 2024 state mandated revaluation. The information
provided in this database is the basis of valuation for each real estate
parcel as it pertains to the 10/1/2024 Grand List. The ownership,
building permit and other parcel information is updated daily from the
assessor's records.

Each parcel's assessment represents
70% of the October 1, 2024 market value. If all or part of a parcel is
classified under P.A. 490 as farm, forest or open space, the land
assessments reflect this use value. Assessment exemptions (for
example, homeowners, veterans and the disabled) ARE NOT REFLECTED in the
final assessment, and will be applied before
tax bills are generated.

Properties with an assessment adjustment for a crumbling foundation are also revalued. The property assessment reflects the reductions made under Public Acts 16-45 and 21-120.

The value for the current grand list year is shown at the top of the record. For the previous value, from the 2018 revaluation, Page Down until you see the Valuation section.

The mill rate for real
estate on the 2025 Grand List is 26.30.

Kara Fishman, Assessor, kfishman@willingtonct.gov
