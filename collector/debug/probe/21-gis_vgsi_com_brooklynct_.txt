Vision Government Solutions

|

BROOKLYN,CT

|

Welcome

Welcome to the Vision Government Solutions Inc. Assessor’s database for
the Town of Brooklyn, CT. The Town of Brooklyn has contracted with Vision
Government Solutions Inc. (formerly known as Vision Appraisal Technology) of Hudson, Massachusetts to assist with the state mandated revaluation project.

Regarding the update schedule: The summary page for each parcel is updated nightly. The attached PDF property record cards are as of October 1, 2024 revaluation and are not updated nightly.
