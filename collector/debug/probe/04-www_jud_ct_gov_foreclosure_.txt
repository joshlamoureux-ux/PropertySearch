Foreclosure Mediation Program - CT Judicial Branch

Judicial Branch
Foreclosure Mediation Program

The Foreclosure Mediation Program was established pursuant to P.A. 08-176 and its purpose is to assist
homeowners and lenders achieve a mutually agreeable resolution to a
mortgage foreclosure action through the mediation process.
