Vision Government Solutions

|

OLD LYME,CT

|

Welcome

The information provided in this
database is the basis of the valuation for each Old Lyme property based on the
10/1/2024 revaluation. Current assessments represent 70% of full market value
as of 10/1/2024.

The mill rate of 16.23 has been set based on the adopted budget for fiscal year 2025/2026.Additional information regarding the mill rate and budget can be found on the Town of Old Lyme web page [https://www.oldlyme-ct.gov/] .

Additional assessment information including contact
information, exemption programs, filing dates, and announcements can be found
on our website at oldlyme-ct.gov [https://www.oldlyme-ct.gov/165/Assessor]

Thank you-

Melinda Kronfeld, CCMA II

Assessor
